you ship a song. we ship the disc.

Make a song in any way you like. We press it onto a real physical CD with your cover art and drop it at your door. Your track also lands on the Hack Club Mixtape compilation everyone gets a copy of.

Join the Slack → find #cut

the deal

you ship

  • an original song, 1–5 min (WAV)
  • cover art you designed
  • your project file + a "how I made it" liner note
  • a Lapse timelapse of your sessions

we ship

  • a physical CD: full colour disc print, jewel case, cellophane wrapped
  • your track on the Hack Club Mixtape compilation

side b — faq

I don't have a DAW. Can I still join?

yes. Sonic Pi is free and code based, Web Audio works in any browser, and you can even record yourself with your phone. any way you make it is fine.

Is AI allowed?

AI assisted is okay, but the song has to be yours: you need the actual project file and a Lapse timelapse of you working on it. if you couldn't open it and edit it yourself, it doesn't count.

What about copyright?

your song must be original: no copyrighted samples, loops, or covers. if it's not yours, it doesn't get pressed.

Does the CD actually play?

yes! a real audio CD that plays in any CD player, car stereo, or discman. full colour printed disc, jewel case with your cover art.

Where does this ship to?

the program is open worldwide. where shipping is unreasonable, you'll still get your track on the mixtape plus a digital copy of the CD.

How long does it take to arrive?

CDs are manufactured within 24 hours of approval and shipped right away! usually a few days after that.
